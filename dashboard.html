Google Sheet

Applications are copied to your own Google Sheet. Changes to status, notes and dates made in the sheet (on your phone, say) come back here when the dashboard opens and every 10 minutes.

How to connect a sheet (5 minutes)
  1. Create a new Google Sheet at sheets.new.
  2. In the sheet: Extensions → Apps Script. Delete the code in the editor, paste the contents of google-sheets/Code.gs and save.
  3. Choose the setup function and press Run. Google asks for access: Advanced → Go to project → Allow. It is your own script and only touches this sheet.
  4. Back in the sheet, open the Job Tracker menu → Show sync token and copy the token.
  5. Deploy → New deployment → type Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Press Deploy and copy the URL ending in /exec.
  6. Paste the URL and token here, press Test connection, then Sync now.

Delete applications here in the extension: a row deleted in the sheet comes back on the next sync. You can add an application in the sheet: fill in Job title and Company on an empty row and it appears in the tracker.
